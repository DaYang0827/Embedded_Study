## 四、第二重点：海外技术支持必考的英语技术问答

下面这8个问题建议你至少能用英语回答到70%的内容。不要逐字死记，理解逻辑后用自己的英语表达。

### Q1. What is an inverter?

逆变器是什么？

> An inverter is a power electronic device that converts DC power into AC power. In a solar energy system, it converts the DC power generated by solar panels into AC power that can be used by household loads or supplied to the grid.

核心词汇： Convert（转换）、DC power（直流电）、AC power（交流电）、Grid（电网）。

追问可能是 What's the difference between a grid-tied inverter and a hybrid inverter?

回答：A grid-tied inverter mainly converts solar DC power into grid-compatible AC power, while a hybrid inverter also supports battery integration and energy management.

### Q2. What is a BMS, and why is it important?

BMS是什么？为什么重要？

> BMS stands for Battery Management System. It monitors battery voltage, current and temperature, estimates the state of charge, and protects the battery from unsafe operating conditions such as overvoltage, overcurrent and overheating.

可能追问： What happens if communication between the BMS and the inverter is lost?

你可以回答：

> The inverter may lose important battery information, such as state of charge and protection status. Depending on the system design, it may stop charging or discharging to protect the battery.

### Q3. What is the difference between AC and DC?

交流电与直流电有什么区别？

> DC flows in one direction, while AC changes direction periodically. Solar panels and batteries provide DC power, while household appliances and power grids commonly use AC power.

准备好后续解释频率、RMS、单相和三相。

### Q4. How does a DC-DC converter work?

DC-DC转换器如何工作？

> A DC-DC converter changes one DC voltage level to another by switching semiconductor devices and transferring energy through inductors, capacitors or transformers. A buck converter reduces voltage, while a boost converter increases voltage.

这是你可以连接 ELEC4410 课程知识的一个好问题。

### Q5. What is the purpose of an LC filter?

LC滤波器有什么作用？

> An LC filter uses an inductor and a capacitor to reduce unwanted frequency components, such as high-frequency switching ripple. In an inverter, it can help produce a smoother output waveform.

特别提醒：整流（Rectification）和滤波（Filtering）是不同的过程。 LC本身不是整流器，你可以借这个机会把之前FAE面试中回答得不够准确的地方补上。

### Q6. What is the difference between CAN and RS485?

CAN和RS485的区别？

> CAN is a communication bus technology that supports multi-node communication, message arbitration and error detection. RS485 mainly defines the electrical characteristics of differential signalling and is often used with protocols such as Modbus RTU.

你可以主动补充：

> In embedded projects, I have experience with UART communication, so I understand concepts like baud rate, data frames and checksums. I would need to learn the specific CAN or Modbus configuration used in Fox ESS products.

这样既能体现技术基础，又不会假装自己已经做过CAN项目。

### Q7. How would you troubleshoot an inverter that is not working?

如果客户反馈逆变器不能正常运行，你怎么处理？

这是非常重要的一题，建议按照以下完整英文答案练习：

> First, I would ask the customer to describe the problem and provide the inverter model, serial number, error code and system status.
> 
> Then, I would review the user manual and check whether the problem is related to the grid, battery, PV input or communication system.
> 
> I would also check the available monitoring data and fault logs.
> 
> If physical inspection or electrical measurements are required, I would arrange for a qualified technician to perform them according to the safety procedures.
> 
> Finally, if I cannot identify the root cause, I would escalate the issue to the engineering team with all relevant information.

其中最重要的一句是：

I would identify the root cause before taking corrective action.

意思是：采取修复措施之前，我会先确定根本原因。

### Q8. How would you handle an angry customer?

客户因为设备故障很生气，你怎么办？

> First, I would listen carefully and acknowledge the customer's concerns. I would stay calm and explain the troubleshooting process clearly.
> 
> I would keep the customer updated and avoid making promises I cannot guarantee.
> 
> If the problem requires further investigation, I would escalate it to the relevant team and follow up until the issue is resolved.

这题主要考英语沟通，而非电气技术。

The project involved packet parsing, ring buffers, Flash programming and CRC verification.
